About the challenge

Build something that matters where you live.

MESA U Hacks 3.0 will bring together Cal State East Bay MESA University students and local community college students for a collaborative, interdisciplinary STEM competition.

Participants will work in assigned teams to design technology-enabled solutions to challenges within their neighborhoods and communities. Winning teams will receive prizes.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

The event is open to Cal State East Bay students and students from participating local community colleges. For Cal State East Bay students: Priority will be given to MESA U members, then any Computer Science, Data Science, or Engineering majors. Students from other majors are welcome to register for the event but will be considered as space allows. If you would like to join CSUEB's MESA U program, you can apply here. For local community college students: Any student from Chabot College, Diablo Valley College or Skyline College, with priority given to MESA students.

Teams will design technology-enabled solutions to challenges within their neighborhoods and communities.

Registration is open now. Complete the registration form to sign up for the event.

No, all projects must be created during the event. The goal of MESA U Hacks is to encourage collaboration and teamwork, so we want to ensure that all participants have an equal opportunity to contribute to their team's project.

Teams will be assigned at the start of the event. We will do our best to ensure that each team has a mix of skills and backgrounds, but we cannot guarantee that you will be placed with specific individuals.

We will provide a light breakfast and lunch on both Saturday and Sunday. A light breakfast will be provided in the MPR on both Saturday and Sunday. Lunch will be at the dining commons on both Saturday and Sunday.

Please bring the following items:

  • A laptop or device capable of coding
  • Your laptop or device charger
  • Any cables or adapters you might need
  • A reusable water bottle
